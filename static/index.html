A LITTLE CONTEXT

Built for people,
and the people helping them.

Daybreak is a synthetic ticket-triage demonstration inspired by first-level People Technology support. It classifies requests, suggests helpful knowledge, and makes its reasoning visible.

It is an independent demo, not a ServiceNow integration or a Cornerstone product. Sensitive requests are flagged for human review. No replies are sent automatically.
